Question 1 of 5

What made you stop scrolling?

What was it about the post or video that made you join the waitlist? What did it name that you'd been feeling?

Question 2 of 5

Where are you now, and where do you want to be?

Describe a normal week in your life right now. Then describe the week you want to be living 12 weeks from now.

Question 3 of 5

What has gotten in the way before?

Think of the last time you committed to a change and it didn't stick. What actually got in the way: time, energy, your environment, other people, your own follow-through?

Question 4 of 5

What does staying the same cost you?

If nothing changes in the next year, what does that cost you in your health, your confidence, your work, or your relationships?
